PLATO v3.5

Deterministic internal generation · numbers and patterns only

    Selections use number frequencies, gaps, relationships, TSUM, individual and set digital roots, Q, population proportions, coverage and overlap scores. No random picks, quick picks, blind picks, seeds, rotation or filler. Equal scores use ascending numbers; unsupported selection stops. For the count rule, P(category) = category count / total: 45/50 = 90% and 5/50 = 10%. This describes a category, not one particular number. Scores are numerical rules, not winning probabilities.
